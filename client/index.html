Built to last

Materials chosen for the parts you touch.

Aluminum where it matters.

Housings, stands, and light bodies use machined aluminum. The load-bearing parts stay stiff, and the surface takes a knock without a plastic scar.

PBT, not paint.

Keycaps are molded PBT with dye-sublimated legends. The texture is the material, so it does not wear through after a year of typing.

Fasteners, not glue.

Parts you can open and replace use screws and standard connectors. The battery, keycaps, and cable are serviceable at a desk.
